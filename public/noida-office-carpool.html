About the route

Most office trips inside Noida and Greater Noida are 10–30 km one way.

Post your ride once with the time you leave home and the time you return. Colleagues from the same society or office can book a seat, and you can keep the same group every day.

How Margshri works

  1. 1
    Owner posts the ride: route, date, time, free seats and per-seat amount.
  2. 2
    Rider books a seat or posts their own request if no ride is listed yet.
  3. 3
    Chat, meet and ride: the phone number is shared only after the booking is accepted. Check each other's ID when you meet.

Questions

How do I start an office carpool group?

Post your daily ride with route, time, seats and per-seat amount. Share the Margshri link in your society or office WhatsApp group so colleagues can book.

Is it only for cars?

No. You can share a bike (one seat), a car or even a bus.

Does Margshri charge any commission?

No. It is free. Riders pay the owner directly the per-seat amount for sharing fuel.

Is my phone number shown to everyone?

No. The phone number is shown to the other person only after a booking is accepted.
