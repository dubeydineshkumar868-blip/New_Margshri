About the route

Pari Chowk to Sector 62 is roughly 25–30 km; Greater Noida West to Sector 62 is roughly 10–15 km.

Most people use the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway (from Pari Chowk / Knowledge Park) or the Greater Noida West link road (from Gaur City / Ek Murti). Office timings are mostly 8–10 am and 6–8 pm, so morning and evening rides fill fastest.

How Margshri works

  1. 1
    Owner posts the ride: route, date, time, free seats and per-seat amount.
  2. 2
    Rider books a seat or posts their own request if no ride is listed yet.
  3. 3
    Chat, meet and ride: the phone number is shared only after the booking is accepted. Check each other's ID when you meet.

Questions

How much does a Greater Noida to Noida carpool cost?

Margshri is cost-sharing only. The car owner sets a per-seat amount to share fuel. As a rough example, a 30 km trip in a car that gives 15 km per litre uses about 2 litres of petrol; shared by 4 people, that is roughly ₹45–50 each. Use the calculator on this page with today's fuel price.

Is there a daily office carpool from Greater Noida West to Sector 62?

Yes, you can post your daily route and time, or search for owners who already posted it. If nobody has posted it yet, post your own request and owners on that route can accept it.

Can I share my bike instead of a car?

Yes. Margshri works for bikes, cars and buses. A bike can share one seat.

Do I need to install an app?

No download from a store is needed. Open margshri.in in Chrome and use “Add to Home screen” to install it like an app.
