About the route

Sector 18 to Cyber City is roughly 40–45 km; Sector 62 to Cyber City is roughly 50–55 km.

Most drivers go via the DND Flyway, Ashram and the Delhi–Gurugram Expressway (NH-48). The trip often takes 1.5 hours or more at peak time, so a fixed group with fixed timings works best.

Questions

How much does a Noida to Gurugram carpool cost?

For a 50 km trip in a car that gives 15 km per litre, fuel is about 3.3 litres; shared by 4 people that is roughly ₹80 each, plus any toll split equally. The owner sets the per-seat amount; it is only to share cost.

Can I set up a regular weekday group?

Yes. Post the ride for each working day at the same time, and riders who book once can chat with you to continue.

What if the driver cancels?

Your booking moves to the Cancelled tab and you can search again or post your own request. Ratings help you choose reliable owners.

Does it work for Greater Noida West to Gurugram?

Yes. Post or search with your exact pickup point; the route is not fixed to these examples.
