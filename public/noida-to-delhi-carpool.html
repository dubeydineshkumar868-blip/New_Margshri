About the route

Sector 18 to Connaught Place is roughly 18–22 km; Sector 62 to Nehru Place is roughly 20–25 km.

Common routes are the DND Flyway towards Ashram and South Delhi, and NH-24 / Noida Link Road towards ITO and central Delhi. Morning traffic peaks around 9–10 am, so agree on a fixed pickup time in chat.

How Margshri works

  1. 1
    Owner posts the ride: route, date, time, free seats and per-seat amount.
  2. 2
    Rider books a seat or posts their own request if no ride is listed yet.
  3. 3
    Chat, meet and ride: the phone number is shared only after the booking is accepted. Check each other's ID when you meet.

Questions

How much does a Noida to Delhi carpool cost?

The owner sets a per-seat amount only to share fuel. For a 22 km trip in a car that gives 15 km per litre, fuel is about 1.5 litres; shared by 4 people that is roughly ₹35–40 each. Use the calculator below with today's fuel price.

Can I find a return ride from Delhi to Noida in the evening?

Yes. Owners usually post both the morning and the evening ride. You can also post your own evening request so drivers going back to Noida can accept it.

Is it allowed to take money for carpooling?

Margshri is for cost-sharing only: the owner shares the running cost and does not earn a profit, like friends splitting fuel.

How do I know the other person is genuine?

You can see ratings and reviews, chat inside the app, and you should check each other's original ID when you meet.
